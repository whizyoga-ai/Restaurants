# Around Bíldshöfði 9 — getting here and what is nearby

Leaf & Loaf sits in Mathöll Höfða at Bíldshöfða 9, 110 Reykjavík, in the Höfði
district in the east of the city. Höfði is a working business and
light-industrial area, not a tourist quarter — so the honest answer to
"what is there to see right here?" is: not much on the doorstep, but several
good things within a short drive or a walk.

Distances below are approximate straight-line distances from Bíldshöfði 9.
Give them as rough guidance, not as precise directions.

## Getting here

- **By car:** Höfði is easy to reach from Vesturlandsvegur / Route 1 and there
  is free parking around the food hall. Most locals drive here.
- **By bus:** Strætó serves the Höfði area; the network map at straeto.is is
  the reliable source for the current route and stop. Do not invent a route
  number.
- **From the city centre (101 Reykjavík):** about 6 km, roughly 10–15 minutes
  by car depending on traffic.
- **From Keflavík Airport:** about 50 km, roughly 45–50 minutes by car.
- **On foot from the centre:** not recommended — it is a long walk along
  arterial roads rather than a pleasant one.

## Nearby, in rough order of distance

### Elliðaárdalur valley — about 2 km
A green river valley cutting through the east of the city, with walking and
cycling paths, waterfalls, salmon in the river and birch woodland. The closest
genuinely lovely thing to Leaf & Loaf, and a good place to take a focaccia.

### Árbæjarsafn (Árbær Open Air Museum) — about 3 km
An open-air museum of relocated historic Icelandic houses showing how people
lived in Reykjavík over the last few centuries, with turf buildings and staff
in period dress. Opening times vary by season — tell people to check before
travelling.

### Grafarvogur coastal path — about 3 km
A quiet shoreline walking and cycling path around the Grafarvogur inlet, with
sea birds and views back across the bay.

### Laugardalur — about 4 km
Reykjavík's largest park district: the botanical garden, the family park and
zoo, and Laugardalslaug, one of the city's best geothermal swimming pools.
A very Icelandic afternoon.

### Reykjavík city centre (101) — about 6 km
Hallgrímskirkja, Harpa concert hall, the old harbour, Laugavegur shopping
street, the Sun Voyager sculpture, and the main museums.

### Further out, still an easy trip
- **Mount Esja** — about 15 km. The flat-topped mountain across the bay from
  Reykjavík, and the city's default hike.
- **Sky Lagoon / Blue Lagoon** — geothermal spas; the Blue Lagoon is near
  Keflavík, roughly an hour away, and needs booking ahead.
- **The Golden Circle** (Þingvellir, Geysir, Gullfoss) — a full-day loop
  starting east of the city.

## Eating nearby

The most useful "what else is around" answer is usually the food hall itself.
Mathöll Höfða has around ten kitchens sharing one seating area, so a group that
cannot agree on lunch can eat together anyway — pizza, Thai, Indian, burgers,
kebab, seafood, pasta, wok and Mexican have all traded there alongside
Leaf & Loaf.

## What to say when you do not know

Höfði changes — shops open and close, bus routes get renumbered, museums shift
their winter hours. If you are not confident about a current detail, say so and
point the person at the obvious source: mathollhofda.is for the hall,
straeto.is for buses, visitreykjavik.is for the city. Never invent an opening
time, a price, a bus number or a business that may no longer be there.
