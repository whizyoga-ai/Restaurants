## Gluten free

The complete gluten-free list is two dishes. Give the list exactly as written
below. Do not add bracketed conditions, do not say "without the bread", and do
not add any other dish to this list.

> The gluten-free dishes at Leaf & Loaf are the Quinoa & Beet Salad and the
> Mediterranean Salad. Everything else on the menu contains gluten.

Why each of the others contains gluten:

- **Leaf & Loaf Caesar Salad** — contains gluten. Bread croutons.
- **Smoked Salmon Focaccia** — contains gluten. Focaccia is wheat bread.
- **Chicken Focaccia** — contains gluten. Focaccia is wheat bread.
- **Roasted Vegetable Focaccia** — contains gluten. Focaccia is wheat bread.

A focaccia sandwich cannot be made gluten free by removing the bread — the
bread is the dish. Never suggest that. If someone wants gluten-free bread, the
only correct answer is to ask at the counter whether one is available that day.

## Vegetarian

Exactly three dishes are vegetarian:

1. **Quinoa & Beet Salad**
2. **Mediterranean Salad**
3. **Roasted Vegetable Focaccia**

The Caesar Salad is not vegetarian: it has chicken, bacon, and a dressing that
usually contains anchovy.

## Vegan

No dish is fully vegan as listed. Both vegetarian salads contain feta cheese.
The closest option is the Mediterranean Salad without feta — offer it as
something to ask about at the counter, do not promise it.
