# Leaf & Loaf — menu, ingredients and nutrition

**How to use the nutrition numbers below.** They are kitchen *estimates*
calculated from the listed ingredients and a standard portion. They are not a
laboratory analysis. Always say they are approximate. If a user mentions an
allergy, an allergen, a medical diet, pregnancy, or anything where being wrong
matters, tell them to confirm with staff at the counter before ordering —
recipes and toppings change with the season.

Prices are in Icelandic krónur (ISK).

---

## Salads

### Leaf & Loaf Caesar Salad — 3,290 ISK
Chicken, iceberg and spinach mix, bacon, bread croutons, lemon, Parmesan,
Caesar dressing.

- Estimated energy: **620 kcal**
- Protein **38 g** · Carbohydrate **24 g** · Fat **40 g**
- Contains gluten (croutons), dairy (Parmesan, dressing), egg (dressing),
  and usually fish (anchovy in Caesar dressing — worth flagging to
  vegetarians and to anyone avoiding fish).
- Not vegetarian. Not gluten free.
- The highest-protein dish on the menu.

### Quinoa & Beet Salad — 3,250 ISK
Roasted beets, quinoa, mixed greens, feta cheese, pistachios,
lemon vinaigrette.

- Estimated energy: **520 kcal**
- Protein **16 g** · Carbohydrate **45 g** · Fat **30 g**
- Vegetarian. Naturally gluten free.
- Contains dairy (feta) and tree nuts (pistachios).
- The best choice for someone who wants vegetarian *and* substantial.

### Mediterranean Salad — 3,150 ISK
Tomatoes, cucumbers, olives, feta, roasted red peppers, olive oil dressing.

- Estimated energy: **380 kcal**
- Protein **11 g** · Carbohydrate **18 g** · Fat **29 g**
- Vegetarian. Naturally gluten free.
- Contains dairy (feta).
- The lightest and the lowest-carbohydrate dish on the menu.

## Focaccia sandwiches

All are served on focaccia baked in house, with seasonal toppings.
Price range across the three: **2,900 – 3,200 ISK**.

### Smoked Salmon Focaccia — 3,200 ISK
- Estimated energy: **560 kcal**
- Protein **27 g** · Carbohydrate **52 g** · Fat **26 g**
- Contains gluten and fish.

### Chicken Focaccia — 3,050 ISK
- Estimated energy: **590 kcal**
- Protein **34 g** · Carbohydrate **53 g** · Fat **25 g**
- Contains gluten.

### Roasted Vegetable Focaccia — 2,900 ISK
- Estimated energy: **470 kcal**
- Protein **13 g** · Carbohydrate **56 g** · Fat **21 g**
- Vegetarian. Contains gluten.
- The least expensive dish on the menu.

Focaccia contains wheat, so none of the sandwiches are gluten free as served.
Ask at the counter whether a gluten-free bread is available that day.

## Drinks

Fresh juices, smoothies, coffee and herbal teas. The focus is healthy and
refreshing rather than indulgent. **Drink prices are not published — say they
are priced at the counter.**

- **Fresh pressed juice** — roughly 120 kcal. Vegan, gluten free.
- **Smoothie** — roughly 230 kcal. Vegetarian; may use a dairy or plant base,
  so ask if it matters.
- **Coffee and herbal tea** — negligible calories black; plant milk on request.

## Quick answers to common questions

- **Cheapest dish:** Roasted Vegetable Focaccia, 2,900 ISK.
- **Most expensive dish:** Leaf & Loaf Caesar Salad, 3,290 ISK.
- **Most protein:** Caesar Salad, about 38 g.
- **Fewest calories:** Mediterranean Salad, about 380 kcal.
- **Vegetarian options:** Quinoa & Beet Salad, Mediterranean Salad,
  Roasted Vegetable Focaccia.
- **Gluten free as served:** Quinoa & Beet Salad, Mediterranean Salad.
- **Vegan:** no dish is fully vegan as listed — the two vegetarian salads both
  contain feta. Ask at the counter whether the feta can be left out; if it can,
  the Mediterranean Salad without feta is the closest vegan option. Do not
  promise this, offer it as something to ask.
- **Nut allergy:** avoid the Quinoa & Beet Salad (pistachios).
- **Fish allergy:** avoid the Smoked Salmon Focaccia, and check the Caesar,
  whose dressing usually contains anchovy.
