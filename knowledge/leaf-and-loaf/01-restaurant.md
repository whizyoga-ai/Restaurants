# Leaf & Loaf — the restaurant

Leaf & Loaf is a fresh-food counter inside **Mathöll Höfða**, a food hall in
Reykjavík, Iceland. It serves vibrant salads, artisan focaccia sandwiches and
fresh drinks. It is casual dining — good for a quick lunch, a solo meal, or a
relaxed bite with a small group.

## Where it is

- **Inside:** Mathöll Höfða (the Höfði food hall)
- **Street address:** Bíldshöfða 9
- **Postcode and city:** 110 Reykjavík
- **Country:** Iceland
- **Neighbourhood:** Höfði, a business and light-industrial district in eastern
  Reykjavík, roughly 6 km from the city centre.

Leaf & Loaf does not have a separate street entrance or its own dining room.
You order at the Leaf & Loaf counter and sit anywhere in the shared food hall
seating.

## Opening hours

**11:30 – 21:00, every day of the week.** These are the food hall's hours and
Leaf & Loaf keeps them.

Iceland does not observe daylight saving time, so local time is UTC all year.

## What kind of food

- Fresh, nutritious, ingredient-led. Not a fine-dining restaurant and not fast
  food — a healthy casual cafe.
- Big salads built to order, focaccia sandwiches on bread baked in house, and
  fresh juices, smoothies, coffee and herbal tea.
- **Vegetarian choices** are available in every category.
- **Gluten-free choices** are available — both salads that are naturally
  gluten free, and gluten-free options on request.
- Popular with tourists looking for something light and healthy, and with
  people working in the surrounding Höfði business district.

## Prices

Most dishes are between **2,900 and 3,290 ISK**. Drinks are priced at the
counter and are not published, so do not quote a drink price — say they are
priced at the counter.

## About the food hall

Mathöll Höfða describes itself as *"Þar sem fólk borðar saman"* — a place where
people eat together. It houses about ten independent kitchens under one roof
with shared seating. Alongside Leaf & Loaf the hall has included Culiacan,
Talay's Pizza, Tandoori Palace, Beef and Buns, WOW Kebab, Skipperinn,
Pasta e Basta, Menam Wok and Gastro Truck. If someone wants pizza, Thai,
Indian, burgers, kebab, seafood, pasta or Mexican, they can get it from another
counter in the same hall — that is a genuinely useful thing to tell a visitor.

The hall's own site is mathollhofda.is.

## Things you must NOT claim

These are not established facts and must never be stated:

- A phone number. None is published.
- A rating, award, or "number one on Tripadvisor" style claim.
- Any second or third location. There is one counter, in Mathöll Höfða.
- Table reservations. It is a food-hall counter; there is no table booking.
- Any address on Laugavegur, in Grandi, or at Kringlan.
- A delivery partner (Wolt, Aha) unless the user says they already use one.

If asked about any of the above, say plainly that you do not have that
information and suggest contacting the food hall or visiting in person.
